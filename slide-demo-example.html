資料來源：Pew Research Center
開場：拋出問題，讓觀眾先試著自己找。不要急著往下。 閱讀挑戰 誰做了什麼事？
Like their peers in traditional full-time employment, freelancers navigating the gig economy and regularly managing multiple short-term projects report that this has had a more positive than negative impact on their overall work-life balance.
強調「34次」這個數字，讓觀眾感受逐字閱讀的低效。這句話剛好34個字，不是隨意捏造的數字。 問題所在 逐字讀，要掃幾次？
逐字閱讀，眼睛要掃 34 次 才能回答「誰做了什麼事」
Like their peers in traditional full-time employment, freelancers navigating the gig economy and regularly managing multiple short-term projects report that this has had a more positive than negative impact on their overall work-life balance.
語塊讀者的眼睛只落幾個地方。接下來的四個動作，帶你切出主幹。
 重點：句首凡是比較、讓步、對比的語塊，一律先切。這是英文句型最常見的開場方式。 第一刀 切掉句首語塊
Like their peers in traditional full-time employment,背景 freelancers navigating the gig economy and regularly managing multiple short-term projects report that this has had a more positive than negative impact on their overall work-life balance.
Like / Although / Despite / In contrast / Apart from…
句首這類語塊一律先切，主幹在它後面。
切掉句首之後，眼睛往右找第一個名詞，那就是主語。這裡是 freelancers。 第二刀 找主語
Like their peers in traditional full-time employment, freelancers主語 navigating the gig economy and regularly managing multiple short-term projects report that this has had a more positive than negative impact on their overall work-life balance.
切掉句首語塊後，第一個名詞 freelancers 就是主語——「誰」做了事。
 後位修飾是中文母語者最容易卡住的地方。中文把修飾語放前面，英文放後面。遇到就跳過，繼續找動詞。 第三刀 跳過後位修飾
Like their peers in traditional full-time employment, freelancers navigating the gig economy and regularly managing multiple short-term projects後位修飾 report that this has had a more positive than negative impact on their overall work-life balance.
主語後緊跟 後位修飾—— 跳過！找後面的動詞。
這是整個拆解過程的高潮。主幹只有三個語塊：freelancers → report → that 子句。其他32個字都是修飾。 主幹浮現 找到動詞

這是視覺化語塊結構的核心技術。縮排代表層次，不需要任何文字說明，結構自己說話。 換行縮排 結構一覽
